You will be provided with course information including:
- Course name and code
- List of course topics/units
- Content summaries from course materials

Create an instant mini-quiz with exactly 10 multiple-choice questions that test students' understanding of the COURSE CONTENT and ACADEMIC CONCEPTS.

## IMPORTANT: Focus on Subject Matter, NOT Administrative Details

❌ **DO NOT ASK ABOUT:**
- Grading policies or percentages
- Assignment due dates
- Course schedule or timeline
- Syllabus structure
- Administrative procedures
- Course logistics

✅ **DO ASK ABOUT:**
- Key concepts and theories from the course topics
- Methodologies and techniques taught in the course
- Applications of course principles
- Problem-solving using course knowledge
- Analysis and interpretation of subject matter
- Definitions of important terms and concepts

## Quiz Structure:

### Quiz Metadata:
- **Title**: Create an engaging, subject-specific title (e.g., "Geospatial Data Science Concepts Quiz")
- **Description**: Brief description focusing on the academic content being tested

### Question Guidelines:
1. **Content Coverage**: Distribute questions across the provided course topics
2. **Difficulty Mix**: 
   - 3-4 easy questions (basic concepts, definitions)
   - 4-5 medium questions (application, analysis)
   - 2-3 hard questions (synthesis, evaluation)
3. **Question Types**:
   - Conceptual understanding
   - Application scenarios
   - Problem-solving
   - Analysis and interpretation

### Format for Each Question:
- **ID**: Sequential number (1-10)
- **Question**: Subject-specific question testing course concepts
- **Options**: Exactly 4 academically relevant answer choices
- **Correct Answer**: Index (0=A, 1=B, 2=C, 3=D) of the correct option
- **Explanation**: Clear explanation of the correct concept/principle
- **Topic**: The specific course topic/unit this question relates to
- **Difficulty**: "easy", "medium", or "hard"
- **Concept**: The specific academic concept being tested

## Content Guidelines:
- Questions should require understanding of course material, not memorization of syllabus details
- Use terminology and concepts specific to the subject area
- Create realistic scenarios that apply course knowledge
- Ensure all options are plausible within the subject domain
- Test understanding of relationships between concepts
- Include questions that require analysis or synthesis of ideas

Generate questions that would help students review and demonstrate mastery of the actual course subject matter and learning objectives.
