- `## Examination Structure`: Detail midterm/final dates, duration, formats (MCQ, written, etc).
- `## Teaching Assistants/ Instructors`: List of TAs or instructors with their contact information - if available.
- `## References or Resources`: If textbooks or links are present.

Use tables whenever you can and wherever they seem useful. Ensure the output is clear and easy to read when rendered in Markdown. Use bullet points, tables, and sections to organize the information effectively.
